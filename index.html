Health Dashboard

A women's health app for iPhone and Apple Watch

What it does: Health Dashboard reads Apple Health and your Apple Watch to work out how much sleep you need, how recovered you are each morning, when your energy peaks and dips, and how your cycle, caffeine and late nights affect you. Everything is compared with your own nights, not a generic average.

Features

  • Sleep need and tonight's bedtime, worked out from your own nights over your first two weeks.
  • A morning recovery score from your sleep, heart rate variability and sleeping heart rate.
  • A smart alarm on Apple Watch that can wake you as you start stirring, with an iPhone backup.
  • Your energy through the day, and a caffeine cutoff that says how much will be left at bedtime.
  • Cycle and birth control, including the pill pack, break week and missed-pill help.
  • Findings about you, like how your recovery changes after earlier bedtimes, from your own data.
  • It fits your life: setup asks about your age, your week and your cycle stage, and the app shows only what applies to you.

Your privacy

Your health data stays on your iPhone and Apple Watch. There are no accounts, servers, ads, analytics or tracking, and the developer never receives your data. Read the full privacy policy.

Calendars

If you're a student, the app can find exams on your calendar and plan your sleep around them. It reads your calendar only, never changes it, and nothing from it leaves your phone. Apple Calendar works today, including Google calendars you've added to your iPhone. Signing in with Google directly is coming in an update: it will ask Google only for read-only access to your calendars, use event titles, dates and times only to find your exams, and keep everything on your phone, as described in the privacy policy.

Health information

Health Dashboard gives general wellness information. It isn't a medical device and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition.
